## After every tested phase

1. Gather the exact implementation revision, test results, screenshots/device observations, unresolved defects and changed decisions.
2. Prepare a phase report and proposed task/phase changes. Leave failed/unrun checks and incomplete work visible.
3. Obtain human approval for the concrete phase report and completion claims. One approval may cover the whole phase update.
4. Apply the approved update through a working Notion connection or manually to existing records. Do not reimport CSV rows as an update mechanism.
5. Re-read the changed records, verify evidence links, and then update the home-page current status and change log.

ChatGPT has no automatic synchronization configured. No recurring automation or background Notion updater was created. A conversation saying “done” does not by itself mean a development phase passed QA.
